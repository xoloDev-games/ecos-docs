# Support

Target response time: **24–48 business hours**. Weekends and holidays
may take longer.

## Before you write

Always include:

1. Exact Unity version (`6000.3.19f1`, for example).
2. Version of `com.unity.ai.inference`.
3. Backend used: `GPUCompute`, `GPUCommandBuffer` or `CPU`.
4. If you use your own model, its version and format (`FP16`, `INT8`, other).
5. The full Console log, not just the last line.
6. Minimal steps to reproduce it.
7. What you expected to happen and what actually happened.

## What support covers

- Unity integration errors.
- questions about configuration, scopes, quantities, memory and reactions;
- problems with model import;
- errors in the documentation.

## What support does not cover

- Full narrative design for your game.
- Third-party AI models other than the recommended one.
- Deep modifications to the Core that break the public API.
- Support for platforms not listed in the requirements.

## Documentation errors

If you find a broken link, a typo or an example that does not compile, open an issue in the
public documentation repository. Include the page and the exact snippet.
